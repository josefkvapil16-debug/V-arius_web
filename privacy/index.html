PRIVACY POLICY

Effective from 13 November 2026

WHAT DATA THE APP USES

With your permission, V-arius reads health and activity data (sleep, steps, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, workouts, weight, menstrual cycle and more) from Apple Health / Health Connect on your phone to estimate fatigue and alertness based on your shift schedule. It can only write back what you enter in the app (workouts, calories burned, cycle entries).

The app also stores what you enter yourself: shift schedule, profile, training plans, notes, equipment. Location is used only to show a workout route on the map, the camera to scan QR codes and take photos, and the gallery only for images and videos you pick or save.

WHERE YOUR DATA STAYS

All data is processed and stored only locally on your phone. The app has no server of its own and no user account. It does not send health or personal data to us, to any third party or for advertising. The calculations are approximate and are not a medical diagnosis.

INTERNET CONNECTION

The app needs the internet only for a few things, and none of them send health or personal data:

  • the map background for a workout route (OpenFreeMap) - the server sees your IP address and the map area being loaded, not your route,
  • the list of public holidays for the calendar (Nager.Date) - only the country and year are sent,
  • videos and links you add to exercises or dances yourself (e.g. YouTube) - they open in that service and follow its rules.

SHARING

Data leaves your phone only when you decide so: exporting or backing up data, saving or sharing a route photo, or passing a workout to a partner via QR code (directly between phones, no server). You decide where the file or photo goes.

DELETING DATA AND PERMISSIONS

Deleting the app from your phone also deletes all data stored in it. Data in Apple Health / Health Connect stays under your control in the system. You can revoke access to it, to location, the camera and photos at any time in your phone settings. You can create or restore a backup at any time in Settings (Data export and backup) - the backup file stays only in the storage you choose.

ADVERTISING AND TRACKING

The app collects no personal data for marketing and contains no ads, analytics tools or third-party tracking.

Усі дані обробляються та зберігаються лише локально у твоєму телефоні. Застосунок не має власного сервера чи облікового запису. Він не надсилає дані про здоров’я чи персональні дані ні нам, ні третім сторонам, ні для реклами. Розрахунки орієнтовні й не є медичним діагнозом.

ПІДКЛЮЧЕННЯ ДО ІНТЕРНЕТУ

Інтернет застосунку потрібен лише для кількох речей, і жодна з них не надсилає даних про здоров’я чи персональних даних:
