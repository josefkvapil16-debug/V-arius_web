The V-arius mobile app (the “app”) is operated by Josef Kvapil, Czech Republic. Questions about the app, its features or this policy, as well as feedback, can be sent to varius.app@gmail.com.

2. DATA PROCESSED

The app processes the following categories of data:

  • health and activity data from Apple Health or Health Connect (e.g. sleep, steps, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, workouts, weight, menstrual cycle), only with consent given in the system permission dialog,
  • data entered in the app (shift schedule, profile, training plans, notes, equipment records),
  • location – only to display a workout route on the map,
  • camera and photo library – only to scan QR codes, take photos and for images and videos you select or save yourself.

The app writes to Apple Health / Health Connect only data entered in the app (workouts, energy burned, cycle entries).

3. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

Data is processed solely to provide the app’s features, in particular estimating fatigue and alertness with regard to shift work and displaying overviews, statistics and training plans. Data is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or behavioural analytics. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Calculations in the app are indicative only and do not constitute a medical diagnosis or advice.

4. STORAGE AND RETENTION

All data is processed and stored exclusively on your device. The app uses no server of the operator and no user account, and the operator has no access to your data.

Data is kept for as long as the app is installed on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored in the app. Data in Apple Health / Health Connect remains managed by the operating system.

5. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The operator does not share or sell user data to anyone. The app connects to the internet only in the following cases, and no health or personal data is sent:

  • OpenFreeMap – loading the map background; the service receives the device’s IP address and the map area being displayed, not the workout route,
  • Nager.Date – loading the list of public holidays; only the country and year are sent,
  • YouTube and other links you add to exercises or dances yourself – they open in the respective service and are subject to its terms.

At your initiative, data may also leave the device when you export or back up data, save or share a route photo, or pass a workout to another user via QR code (directly between devices, without a server). You decide where an exported file is stored.

6. PERMISSIONS AND WITHDRAWAL

The app accesses health data, location, camera and photos only with your consent through system permissions. You can withdraw granted permissions at any time in the device settings or in the Apple Health / Health Connect settings. Withdrawing permissions may limit some features of the app.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Because all data is stored only on your device, you have full control over it: you can view, edit, export (Settings → Data export and backup) or delete it at any time, including by uninstalling the app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. CHILDREN

The app is not intended for children under 16. The operator does not collect data from any users, including children.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

The operator may update this policy, in particular in connection with new features of the app. The current version is always available in the app and at www.v-arius.cz/privacy. The effective date is shown at the top.
